A3-120 ENGINEERING PROTOTYPE. Not a vehicle-qualified manufacturing release.
Nominal PCB outline: 120 x 120 mm. Thickness: 1.6 mm.
Six copper layers, in order: F.Cu / In1.Cu GND / In2.Cu signal / In3.Cu signal / In4.Cu GND / B.Cu.
Two uninterrupted ground planes, except necessary pad clearances and module antenna keepouts. Do not merge or omit internal layers.
Use the native KiCad project, BOM and final ERC/DRC reports for review. Discuss the actual copper weights/dielectrics, stack thickness and drilling capabilities with the fabricator before ordering. No controlled impedance or qualified output-current/temperature rating is claimed.
Minimum design trace/clearance is 0.2 mm; board-edge copper rule is 0.4 mm. Vias are tented. PTH and NPTH drill files are separate. Component holes are for ordinary leaded solder joints.
Generic passive BOM entries still require exact purchased-part selection and physical review. Check all mating plugs, printed case, solder-tail reliefs and harness routing before assembly. Native CAD checks do not establish physical fit, EMC, automotive surge immunity or operation on an engine.
