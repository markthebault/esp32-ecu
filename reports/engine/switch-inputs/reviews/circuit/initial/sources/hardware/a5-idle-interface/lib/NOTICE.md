The `.kicad_mod` files in `ecu.pretty` are copied from the installed KiCad 10 library. Their original generator, descriptions and model paths are retained. See the [KiCad library license and exception](https://www.kicad.org/libraries/license/) and the original [KiCad footprints repository](https://gitlab.com/kicad/libraries/kicad-footprints).

The schematic symbols in `ecu.kicad_sym` are generated by this project's `scripts/build_hardware.py`. Their pin maps must be reviewed against the exact manufacturer part, package and order code before assembly.
