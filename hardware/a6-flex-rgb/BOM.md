# A6 development BOM, fabrication on hold

The native contract contains 264 footprints and 249 fitted electronic references. The [grouped draft shopping CSV](bom-shopping.csv), [per-reference assembly CSV](bom.csv) and [carrier pinout](connector-pinout.csv) describe this separate draft.

All listed MPNs are candidates. Match actual power, tolerance, temperature, ESR, lead pitch, upright formed body and height. Direct-solder DIP devices; only the ESP32 uses sockets. The old enclosure/material BOM remains historical and does not establish A6 fit. The PCB has no fabrication release.

J22 sensor supply is not18V limited, and its external sensor pinout is unresolved. J23 PH5 has four populated contacts; pin5 remains empty. R179330R limits its common supply in addition to the three cathode resistors; the conditional ground-fault bound does not establish brightness or a physical protection rating. R179 is the upright330R MRS16 candidate on its smaller DIN0204 footprint. Match the stated body/rating, formed leads and actual assembly; thermal and case fit remain unqualified. The RGB supervisor is MCP121-270E/TO; release hysteresis has a25C table condition. Rail headroom, loaded GPIO/readback, reset, heat, optics and sealed installation require physical checks.

The compact frontend uses nine MRS16 0.4W P70 resistor candidates, R157 through R165. Their body envelope is a maximum3.6 x1.6mm; lead forming, assembled derating and heat remain unqualified. R155/R156/R166 retain their750R 0.6W selection. C158/C159 use C315 C0G parts; C160 and the added local3V3 bypass C164 use C315 X7R parts. Nominal100nF is not an effective-value or supply-impedance guarantee. The proposed0.8mm holes and2.54mm pitch require checking actual leads and formed pitch; the specified pitch tolerance can require forming. Use the manufacturer hand-solder preheat/thermal-shock guidance. J20 uses B2B-PH-K-S(LF)(SN), PHR-2 and two SPH-002T-P0.5S contacts for the internal CHASSIS shield/lining bond, with no normal load current. H5 remains the chassis lug. These component/package changes do not establish enclosure, module, electrical or physical fit.
